Oblique Spaceship Demo

Spaceship list CA notation and normalization

The supplied initial configuration runs on a torus. Edit the rules or sequence to explore its behavior. Reset restores an explicit pattern or generates a fresh random board at the specified density. The trajectory follows the circular centroid.

Single-rule cellular automata are also supported: define one rule and use Seq 1 to apply it every generation, with either an explicit pattern or random initialization.
